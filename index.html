Profile

Physician first. Strategist by necessity.

I am an emergency physician and trauma & hand surgeon with more than twenty years of hospital and pre-hospital practice. Since the early 2000s I have worked at system level: I was the principal architect of Hungary's renewed helicopter emergency medical service, and served as its director between 2005 and 2011.

After leaving the public sector I moved to the international market. Between 2011 and 2015 I worked as an expert consultant and project manager on aeromedical and air-ambulance programmes from London through Nuremberg and Pristina to Dubai — already building with my own company. In 2015, after years of preparation, we founded TrustAir Aviation Ltd., which has grown into a recognised European fixed-wing air ambulance operator.

Today my work combines clinical credibility with operational and strategic leadership: designing aeromedical services, advising operators, governments and insurers, training crews, and speaking at international conferences. I continue to publish, lecture and support the profession — as publisher and patron of three specialist textbooks in Hungary, and as a board member of the Vízimentésért Foundation.

What I do

I help organisations that need proven aeromedical expertise — operators, regulators, insurers, hospitals and training providers — to design safer, faster and more sustainable medical aviation systems.
